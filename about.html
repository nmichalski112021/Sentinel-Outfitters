About

Who we are.

Sentinel Outfitters LLC is a small shop in Gulfport, Mississippi. We design and 3D print optic accessories and everyday-carry gear in-house, in small batches.

The shop

Sentinel Outfitters is owned and run by Nick (Nicholas Michalski). Every part is designed, 3D printed, checked, and packed by us in Gulfport, Mississippi, then shipped from Gulfport to US addresses.

We sell accessories only — honeycomb anti-reflection covers (killflash ARDs) and small EDC items. We do not sell firearms or any FFL-regulated items.

How we make it

Printed in-house, checked by hand.

We print on our own FDM 3D printers in Gulfport, in PLA+, PETG, or carbon-fiber nylon depending on the part.

Before a part ships, we check its fit on the critical dimensions for that SKU, clean up the edges, and look it over. If a part does not pass, we reprint it.

Fit notes

Our optic accessories are made for specific models. Other optics — even ones with the same nominal size — vary in outside diameter and may not fit. Measure your objective's outside diameter and message us before ordering if your optic is not the exact model listed.

For example, the Diamondback 10x50 pair is made for the Vortex Diamondback 10x50. Other Vortex 50mm binoculars, including Diamondback HD and Crossfire models, may not fit.
