The Shop

What “100% inspected” means

Nothing ships because it came off the bed looking close enough. Before a part leaves Gulfport it is checked against the spec for that SKU:

  • Dimensional check against printed tolerance (± 0.05mm on critical fits)
  • Layer adhesion and infill continuity on load-bearing walls
  • Hardware seats, rail geometry, and mounting holes cycled by hand
  • Edges hand-deburred; no sharp flash on contact surfaces
  • Visual finish pass under shop light before bagging

If a part fails any of those, it is reprinted. We do not sell seconds.
