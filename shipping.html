Shipping & returns.

Direct from the shop in Gulfport, Mississippi. Inspected before it leaves. Built to outlast the shift — and packed that way too.

Shipping

Orders ship from Gulfport, Mississippi after a 100% inspection pass. Lead times depend on the run — in-stock accessories typically leave within a few business days; made-to-order parts follow the quote.

All orders ship in compliance with applicable federal, state, and local law. Some products may be restricted in your jurisdiction — it's on you to check before you buy.

Returns

If a part arrives damaged or out of spec, contact us with photos and the order details. Defective items are replaced or refunded. Custom geometry that was built to your measurements is not returnable unless we missed the spec.

Age requirement

You must be 18 or older to purchase from Sentinel Outfitters LLC.

Privacy

We use the email you give us to answer your dispatch or send Watch updates you asked for. We do not sell your information.
